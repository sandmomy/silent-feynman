Destination 01 / 04Location

Yogya­karta

The cultural heart of Java — where 9th-century temples radiate cosmic frequencies and sacred traditions still flow through everyday life.

🕌
Borobudur
The Frequency of Harmony

Built in the 9th century, this masterpiece is designed as a cosmic mandala. Its bell-shaped stupas radiate natural frequencies, creating a flow of harmony and positive frequency. Walking its concentric terraces is a meditation in itself — each step a calibration.

⚡
Prambanan
Temple of Balance and Movement

Dedicated to the Trimurti — Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva. The temple's sacred frequency makes it a powerful place for yoga practice. The spires reach toward the sky like antennae tuned to the cosmos, channeling divine balance into those who seek it.

🌊
Beach & Silat
The Spirit of Flow

Rooted in Javanese tradition, Silat teaches balance, discipline, and the flow of frequency between body and nature. Practice by the sea where the waves set the rhythm — movement as meditation, breath as compass.

Destination 02 / 04Location

Malang & Batu

The land of the Sultan's — one of the most spiritual places in the world. Encircled by sacred volcanoes, where luxury meets untouched nature.

🏨
Shanaya Wellness Resort
Our Base — 5-Star Luxury

In the heart of Batu Malang, surrounded by the sacred mountains Bromo, Semeru, Arjuna, and Gunung Kawi. Where luxury and nature are perfectly balanced — your sanctuary between journeys into the wild.

🌋
Bromo
The Sacred Volcano of Frequencies

Feel the frequency of the volcano, as the earth's subtle vibration flows through your body and soul. Standing at the crater's edge, you sense the planet breathing — raw, primordial, and deeply humbling.

⛩️
Gunung Kawi
The Mountain of Living Frequencies

A sacred temple mountain where offerings and meditation are dedicated to wealth, prosperity, and health. The living frequencies of prayer and intention have saturated this place for centuries.

🏔️
Semeru
Yoga on the Mountain of Mystical Power

The highest peak in Java, revered as the sacred axis of the island. Yoga practice here connects earth and sky — where breath merges with mountain air and time dissolves into stillness.

💧
Tumpak Sewu
The Waterfall of Flowing Frequencies

Its countless streams cascade like a living curtain of light and sound. The negative ion field of the falls is deeply restorative — sound meditation here is unlike anything else on earth.

🌙
Virgin Beaches
Waves of Living Frequency

Camp under the stars, practice yoga by the ocean, sound meditation in sea caves with traditional gamelan. The untouched coastline carries an ancient stillness — the ocean as teacher, the stars as guide.

Destination 03 / 04Location

Bali

The Island of the Gods — where dolphins carry healing frequencies and sacred water temples hold centuries of prayer.

🐬
Lovina & the Dolphins
Dolphins of Frequency

These gentle beings are teachers of frequency and harmony. Their ultrasonic waves carry healing, balance, and joy — swimming alongside them is a direct transmission of living frequency. One of the most transformative experiences of the entire journey.

🌊
Sacred Waters
Water Temple Rituals

Balinese water purification rituals at ancient temples. The sacred springs of Bali have been used for millennia to cleanse body, mind, and spirit — a reset for your entire energetic field.

Destination 04 / 04Location

Sumba

The Island of Horses — where spirit and nature ride together in a landscape untouched by time.

🐎
Horses of Sumba
The Island of Horses

Swim with the horses in the sea — feel their strength, their wildness, their ancient spirit. Sumba is where spirit and nature ride together. The horses here are sacred, and their frequency is transformative. Nothing prepares you for this.

🌅
Wild Landscapes
Untouched by Time

Rolling savannahs, dramatic cliffs, and villages where megalithic traditions live on. Sumba's raw beauty is a final mirror — reflecting the transformation of your entire journey back at you.
