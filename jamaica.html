Community Pilot Program

Jamaica Pilot Project

Transforming communities through movement, discipline, and human connection in the heart of the Caribbean.

01 / The Vision

A Blueprint for Change

The Jamaica Pilot Project is Eugene Mierak's hands-on initiative to empower underserved communities through structured physical training, mindfulness, and mentorship. Set in the mountains of Jamaica, the program brings together children, families, and local leaders to build discipline, resilience, and lasting bonds through the universal language of movement.
