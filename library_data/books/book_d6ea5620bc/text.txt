Chapter 3 
 
Life Frequency: Applying Frequency Memory in Daily Life 
 
Introduction 
Life Frequency means actively using learned frequency memories in real-time situations. 
It is the moment where awareness, choice, and embodiment come together. 
You are no longer reacting automatically. 
You are selecting a frequency. 
Every situation has a dominant frequency: 
• Conflict 
 
• Stress 
 
• Cooperation 
 
• Fear 
 
• Creativity 
 
• Leadership 
 
• Calm 
 
When you recognize the frequency of a situation, you can enter it consciously—not as a 
victim of circumstance, but as a regulator of the field. 

 
 1. Frequency Awareness: Reading the Situation 
Before action comes perception. 
Frequency awareness is the ability to notice: 
• Tempo (fast / slow) 
 
• Tension (tight / open) 
 
• Direction (chaotic / coherent) 
 
• Emotional charge (fear, aggression, calm) 
 
This happens before words. 
Once you sense the frequency, you gain choice. 
 
2. Frequency Storage: Your Inner Toolkit 
Through training with nature (waves, trees, stone, wind), you have stored: 
• Rock frequency → stability, firmness, presence 
 
• Bamboo frequency → flexibility, adaptability 
 
• Water frequency → flow, emotional intelligence 
 
• Wind frequency → speed, lightness, perspective 
 
• Fire frequency → activation, clarity, drive 
 
Life Frequency is choosing which one to activate. 
 
3. Frequency Transmission: What You Send Out Shapes What Returns 
Frequency is contagious. 
• Nervous systems mirror each other 
 
• Emotional states entrain 
 
• Groups follow the strongest coherent rhythm 
 
When you change your frequency: 
• You change the interaction 
 
• You change the outcome 
 
• You change what frequency comes back to you 

 
 
Practical Life Frequency Examples 
 
1. De-escalating a Threatening Situation 
 
Situation 
Someone is aggressive, loud, emotionally charged. 
Common Reaction 
• Matching aggression 
 
• Freezing 
 
• Escalation 
 
Life Frequency Choice 
Mountain / Rock Frequency 
How 
• Slow your breath 
 
• Lower your voice 
 
• Reduce movement 
 
• Ground your posture 
 
Effect 
• The other nervous system has nothing to fight 
 
• Aggression loses resonance 
 
• Situation de-escalates naturally 
 
You do not overpower the situation. 
You out-stabilize it. 

 
 
2. Conflict at Work or in a Relationship 
Situation 
Tension, disagreement, emotional rigidity. 
Life Frequency Choice 
Water Frequency 
How 
• Listen fully before responding 
 
• Use soft but continuous speech 
 
• Allow pauses 
 
• Stay emotionally mobile 
 
Effect 
• Resistance dissolves 
 
• Emotions move instead of clash 
 
• Solutions emerge organically 
 
Water does not argue with stone. 
It reshapes the landscape. 
 
3. Motivating Employees or a Team 
Situation 
Low energy, disengagement, lack of initiative. 
Live Frequency Choice 
Fire + Wind Frequency 
How 
• Upright posture 
 
• Clear, concise language 
 
• Rhythmic speech 
 
• Vision-oriented communication 
 
Effect 
• Energy rises 
 
• Attention sharpens 
 
• Momentum builds 
 
People follow coherent energy, not authority alone. 

 
 4. Public Speaking, Lectures, Presentations 
Situation 
Audience uncertainty, pressure, performance stress. 
Live Frequency Choice 
Tree Frequency (Rooted + Open) 
How 
• Ground feet like roots 
 
• Open chest like branches 
 
• Breathe steadily 
 
• Speak from stillness 
 
Effect 
• Voice becomes stable 
 
• Audience relaxes 
 
• Trust increases 
 
• Presence speaks louder than information. 

 
 5. Solving Complex Problems 
Situation 
Mental overload, confusion, analysis paralysis. 
Live Frequency Choice 
Stone → Wind Transition 
How 
1. First: become still (stone) 
 
2. Slow thoughts 
 
3. Then: zoom out (wind) 
 
4. Change perspective 
 
Effect 
• Noise drops 
 
• Patterns appear 
 
• Insight emerges 
 
• Clarity comes from frequency reduction, not force. 
 
 
6. Creating Peace, Love, and Understanding 
Situation 
Emotional distance, misunderstanding, division. 
Live Frequency Choice 
Heart-Coherent Wave Frequency 
How 
• Slow breathing 
 
• Gentle tone 
 
• Open facial expression 
 
• Non-threatening rhythm 
 
Effect 
• Defensive barriers drop 
 
• Empathy activates 
 
• Connection restores 
 
Love is not an emotion. 
It is a coherent frequency state. 

 
 7. Personal Stress or Emotional Overload 
Situation 
Anxiety, racing thoughts, emotional pressure. 
Live Frequency Choice 
Wave or Forest Frequency 
How

• Gentle repetitive movement 
 
• Reduce stimulation 
 
Effect 
• Nervous system resets 
 
• Emotional balance returns 
 
• Self-regulation restored 
 
• You become your own environment. 
 
 
8. Leadership Under Pressure 
Situation 
Crisis, uncertainty, people looking for direction. 
Live Frequency Choice 
Mountain + Fire (Still Authority) 
How 
• Minimal movement 
 
• Clear decisions 
 
• Calm but decisive tone 
 
Effect 
• Others stabilize 
 
• Trust increases 
 
• Chaos organizes around you 
 
True leadership is frequency regulation. 

 
 Conclusion 
Live Frequency is not acting. 
It is being deliberately. 
You do not ask: 
“What should I do?” 
You ask: 
“What frequency does this moment require?” 
 
Through awareness, training, and frequency memory: 
• You choose what you consume 
 
• You choose what you emit 
 
• You influence what returns 
 
 
You become adaptive without losing integrity. 
Strong without aggression. 
Soft without weakness. 
You are no longer reacting to life.
