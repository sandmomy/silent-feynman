Part 1: The Daily F × A Planner 
Core Principle 
Don’t plan only your actions. 
Plan the frequency in which those actions are executed. 
 
1. Morning Alignment (Setting the Frequency) 
Time: 5–15 minutes 
Step 1 — Check-In 
Ask: 
• What is my current state? 
• Fast or slow? 
• Tense or relaxed? 
• Focused or scattered? 
 
Step 2 — Choose Your Frequencies (F) 
Select 1–3 frequencies for the day: 
Examples: 
• Mountain → stability, leadership 
• Water → flexibility, communication 
• Fire → execution, productivity 
• Wind → creativity, strategy 

 
 
Step 3 — Define Key Actions (A) 
Choose 3–5 core actions: 
• Meetings 
• Calls 
• Training 
• Problem-solving 
• Creative work 
 
Step 4 — Link F × A 
Match each action with a frequency: 
Example: 
• Meeting → Mountain (calm authority) 
• Creative task → Wind (open thinking) 
• Workout → Fire (intensity)

2. Midday Reset (Recalibration) 
Time: 2–5 minutes 
Check: 
• Am I still in the chosen frequency? 
• Did I drift into stress, urgency, distraction? 
 
Reset Method: 
• Slow breath (wave) 
• Ground posture (mountain) 
• Relax shoulders 
• Re-enter chosen frequency. 

 
 3. Evening Reflection (Integration) 
Time: 5–10 minutes 
Ask: 
• Where did F × A align? 
• Where did I lose frequency? 
• What result (M) did I create? 
 
Write down: 
• 1 success 
• 1 improvement 
• 1 frequency to refine tomorrow 
 
 
Part 2: Frequency Training Curriculum 
 
Overview 
This curriculum builds: 
1. Awareness 
2. Control 
3. Application 
4. Mastery 
 
 
Phase 1: Awareness (Week 1–2) 
Goal: Recognize frequency in yourself and others 
Training: 
• Observe your state 3x per day 
• Label frequencies: 
• Fast / slow 
• Open / closed 
• Calm / reactive 
 
Practice: 
Sit quietly → notice internal rhythm 
Watch people → detect their frequency 
Outcome: 
You stop reacting blindly. 

 
 
Phase 2: Regulation (Week 3–4) 
Goal: Control your internal frequency 
Training: 
Daily 10–15 min: 
• Slow breathing (wave) 
• Still posture (mountain) 
• Gentle movement (water) 
 
 
Practice: 
Switch states intentionally: 
• Calm → active 
Active → calm 
Outcome: 
You gain control over your nervous system. 
 
Phase 3: Frequency Memory (Week 5–6) 
Goal: Store and recall frequencies 
 
Training: 
Practice with natural models: 
• Wave → breathing rhythm 
• Tree → balanced posture 
• Fire → controlled intensity 
 
Exercise: 
Recreate the state without external input. 
Outcome: 
You carry frequencies internally. 
Phase 4: Application (Week 7–8) 
Goal: Use F × A in real situations 
Training: 
Daily planner usage 
Practice: 
Apply in: 
• Conversations 
• Work tasks 
Stress situations 
Outcome: 
You influence outcomes consciously. 

 
 
Phase 5: Mastery (Ongoing) 
Goal: Automatic frequency selection 
Training: 
• Real-time awareness 
Instant switching 
Practice: 
• High-pressure situations 
• Leadership roles 
Complex environments 
Outcome: 
You become a reference frequency for others.
