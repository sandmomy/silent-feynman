About the University

A Leading University in Indonesia

UNISMA (Universitas Malang) is a prestigious university located in Malang, East Java, Indonesia. Founded under the Nahdlatul Ulama (NU) tradition, it combines modern academic excellence with strong cultural values.

With "Unggul" (Excellent) accreditation from BAN-PT and international ASIIN & FIBAA accreditations, UNISMA is recognized globally for its commitment to quality education, research, and community empowerment.

Discover Malang

Why Study in Malang?

Nestled between sacred volcanoes and lush highlands, Malang offers the perfect blend of academic life, culture, and natural beauty.

🌋 Sacred Mountains

Surrounded by Mount Bromo, Semeru, Arjuna, and Gunung Kawi — some of Indonesia's most iconic volcanic landscapes. Perfect for weekend exploration.

💰 Affordable Living

One of the most affordable cities in Java for students. Delicious local food, comfortable housing, and transportation at a fraction of big-city costs.

🌿 Cool Highland Climate

At 440m altitude, Malang enjoys cooler temperatures than most Indonesian cities — ideal for focused studying and comfortable daily life.

🎭 Rich Cultural Heritage

A city where Javanese and Dutch colonial heritage converge. Temples, traditions, and a vibrant arts scene define everyday life.

🎓 Student City

Home to over 80 higher education institutions. A true student city with cafés, co-working spaces, and a thriving academic community.

🌏 Gateway to East Java

Strategic access to Bali, Surabaya, and the stunning beaches of East Java's southern coast. International airport with growing connectivity.
