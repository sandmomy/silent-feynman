Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Eugene Mierak, operating under the brand Frequency Vibes ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you visit eugenemierak.com and book.eugenemierak.com (together, the "Sites").

We are committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on Personal Data Protection (LOPD-GDD), and the Spanish Law 34/2002 on Information Society Services (LSSI-CE).

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data is:

  • Name: Eugene Mierak
  • Brand: Frequency Vibes
  • Email: info@eugenemierak.com

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Data you provide directly

  • Contact forms: name, email address, phone number, and message content
  • Account registration (book.eugenemierak.com): email address, name, and password
  • Payments: when you purchase services or digital content, your payment details are processed by Stripe. We do not store your card details.
  • Booking requests: name, email, country, number of guests, and notes

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL
  • Usage data: pages visited, time spent, interactions
  • Cookies: session cookies for authentication and site functionality (see Section 7)

3. Why We Process Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide our services: process bookings, deliver digital content, manage your account (Legal basis: contract performance)
  • To process payments: handle transactions via Stripe (Legal basis: contract performance)
  • To communicate with you: respond to inquiries, send booking confirmations (Legal basis: legitimate interest / contract)
  • To improve our sites: analyze usage patterns, fix bugs (Legal basis: legitimate interest)
  • To comply with legal obligations: tax, accounting, fraud prevention (Legal basis: legal obligation)

4. Who We Share Data With

We only share your personal data with:

  • Stripe, Inc. — payment processing (Stripe Privacy Policy)
  • Vercel, Inc. — website hosting
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — DNS, CDN, and domain services
  • Google — if you use Google OAuth to sign in

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary:

  • Account data: for as long as your account is active, plus 30 days after deletion request
  • Payment records: up to 7 years for tax and legal compliance
  • Contact form submissions: up to 12 months
  • Technical logs: up to 90 days

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict processing
  • Data portability — receive your data in a portable format
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@eugenemierak.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory data protection authority. In Spain, this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD): www.aepd.es

7. Cookies

We use the following cookies:

  • Session cookies: essential for login and authentication on book.eugenemierak.com. These expire when you close your browser or after 24 hours.
  • Stripe cookies: set by Stripe during the checkout process for fraud detection and payment security.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. International Transfers

Some of our service providers (Stripe, Vercel, Cloudflare) may process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). These transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or adequacy decisions as required by the GDPR.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including HTTPS encryption, secure authentication, and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. For significant changes, we will notify registered users by email.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact:

Email: info@eugenemierak.com
