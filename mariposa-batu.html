The Vision

A New Model for Integrated Living in Indonesia

Mariposa at Klub Bunga is positioned within the Batu–Malang–Mount Bromo corridor — one of Indonesia's most iconic highland destinations. The project introduces a first-of-its-kind concept: a fully integrated Natural Medical Wellness & Senior Living Resort.

Batu City is being positioned as a National Main Medical & Wellness Center, attracting over 7.2 million visitors annually with its cool climate, volcanic landscapes, and world-class hospitality infrastructure.

Combining cutting-edge medical services, holistic wellness programs, premium senior living communities, and integrated tourism — all within one master-planned development spanning 372,000 m².

Development Roadmap

Master Plan

A phased development approach across 372,000 m² ensuring controlled growth and sustainable returns.

Phase 1.0 — The Nodes

Central Hub & Premium Villas

  • Mariposa Central Node — multi-story hub for clinics, wellness & senior living
  • Standard Apartments — ~45 sqm units
  • Deluxe Apartments — ~60 sqm units
  • Suite Apartments — ~85 sqm units
  • Executive Apartments — ~120 sqm units
  • 29 Premium Villas — standalone luxury with infinity pools & mountain views
