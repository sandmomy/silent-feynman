Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Eugene Mierak, operating under the brand Frequency Vibes / BookVoice ("we", "us"), collects, uses, and protects your personal data on book.eugenemierak.com (the "Platform").

1. Data Controller

Eugene Mierak — Email: info@eugenemierak.com

2. Data We Collect

Account data

  • Email address, name, and password (hashed)
  • Google account info if you sign in with Google

Purchase data

  • Books purchased and access grants
  • Payment details processed by Stripe (we never store card numbers)

Usage data

  • Pages visited, reading progress, audio playback
  • IP address, browser type, device information

3. Why We Process Data

  • Provide access to purchased books and audio (contract performance)
  • Manage your account and authentication (contract performance)
  • Process payments via Stripe (contract performance)
  • Improve the platform and fix issues (legitimate interest)
  • Legal compliance: tax, accounting (legal obligation)

4. Data Sharing

We share data only with: Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (hosting/CDN), and Google (if you use Google sign-in). We do not sell your data.

5. Cookies

We use session cookies for authentication (expire after 24 hours or on logout). No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

You can access, correct, delete, or export your data at any time. Contact info@eugenemierak.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD): www.aepd.es

7. Data Retention

  • Account data: for as long as your account is active, plus 30 days after deletion request
  • Purchase records: up to 7 years for tax and legal compliance
  • Usage data (reading progress, playback): for as long as your account is active
  • Technical logs: up to 90 days

8. International Transfers

Some of our service providers (Stripe, Cloudflare) may process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). These transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or adequacy decisions as required by the GDPR.

9. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, signed session tokens, and access controls. No system is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

Eugene Mierak — info@eugenemierak.com
