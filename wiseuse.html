Our Sectors

Three Pillars, One Philosophy

Every solution we develop honours the same principle: activate what nature already provides. We apply this across agriculture, water management, and animal health.

Agriculture

Restoring soil health from the microbiome up. Our biological activators stimulate plant growth, strengthen natural pest resistance, and optimize crop performance without synthetic inputs. Healthier soil means healthier food, and a healthier planet.

Water Management

Cleaning contaminated lagoons, rivers, ponds, and industrial water systems. Our solutions remove harmful algae, reduce bacterial loads, degrade oil pollution, and restore biological clarity, allowing aquatic ecosystems to regenerate naturally.

Animal Health

Supporting intestinal health in poultry and livestock through biological solutions that strengthen natural gut flora. Reducing dependency on antibiotics while improving animal welfare, feed efficiency, and overall production quality.

Connected by one truth: nature already has the intelligence to heal itself.

Global Impact

Decades of Measurable Restoration

From South American lakes to Southeast Asian universities, our biological solutions have demonstrated consistent, verifiable environmental results.

Lake Ypacarai Restoration

Paraguay 2007

Coliform counts reduced from 1,500 to 200. Cyanobacteria populations substantially decreased. Zooplankton recovery confirmed restored biodiversity across the entire lake ecosystem.

Lake Maracaibo Oil Cleanup

Venezuela 2013

In partnership with PDVSA, achieved a 40–50% decrease in suspended oil content. Treated samples showed significantly faster spontaneous biological degradation compared to control areas.

Tiscapa Lagoon Management

Nicaragua 2020

The 0.4 km² lagoon was rendered free of harmful blue-green algae within 3 months. Young fish populations returned as water clarity and health were restored.

University Research Collaboration

Indonesia 2024

Active research partnerships with Indonesian universities advancing knowledge exchange, validating biological solutions through academic rigour, and promoting sustainable agricultural development.
