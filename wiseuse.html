Restoring Balance Through Nature

At Wise Use International, we believe nature knows best. Our mission is to restore vitality and balance in soil, water, plants, animals, and humans — by harnessing the power of naturally occurring microorganisms.

We develop and produce biological products that stimulate plants to grow better, fight plagues, clean water, and optimize composting. Our philosophy is to restore balance by activating existing microorganisms.

International Research Projects

Case studies and feasibility reports from around the globe

Lake Ypacarai Restoration

Paraguay 2007

Substantial decrease in coliforms (1500→200) and cyanobacteria. Increased zooplankton indicating restored biodiversity.

Lake Maracaibo Oil Cleanup

Venezuela 2013

PDVSA partnership: 40-50% decrease in suspended oil. Faster spontaneous reaction demonstrated in treated samples.

Tiscapa Lagoon Management

Nicaragua 2020

0.4 km² lagoon almost free of blue/green algae in 3 months. Young fish spotted due to increased clarity.
