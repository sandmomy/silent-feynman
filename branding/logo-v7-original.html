A tiny eye at the dead center — a hollow gold dot. Subtle nod to your original Anubis idea, without making the mark literal.

Small radial lines at 4 cardinal points and 8 secondary positions. The disk now reads as a tuner, a dial — a frequency.

The complex closed spiral becomes two open hooks — readable, modern, scales to favicon. The cosmos as a sound-wave gesture.

Two thin black curves inside each diagonal sector. From a distance: ornament. Up close: tiny waves. They make the disk vibrate.

05 · CURLS

Refined cardinal curls

The hooks at the end of the four arms are now tighter and more elegant — less folkloric, more editorial. Same DNA, refined silhouette.
