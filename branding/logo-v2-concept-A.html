05

Do & Don't

Five rules. Anyone touching the mark — designer, printer, agency — reads these first.

DO

Respect the clear space

Always leave breathing room around the glyph equal to the height of one corner triangle. No element invades.

DO

Pair with editorial type

Cormorant Garamond or Fraunces for the wordmark. IBM Plex Mono for accents. No sans-serif geometric.

DO

Use gold on dark first

The primary lock-up is always preferred. Inverse and mono only when the medium forces it.

DON'T

Don't recolor on a whim

No purples, no neon, no pastels. Gold #D4A866 or its monochrome translations. Period.

DON'T

Don't stretch or rotate

The mark is square. Never distort proportions or rotate beyond 0°. The cosmos is balanced.

DON'T

Don't drop below 24px

For the full glyph. Below that, switch to the favicon-simplified variant (frame + eye only).
