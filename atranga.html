A water-based nano-coating that fuses with the surface — invisible, durable, hydrophobic. Protection becomes part of the material itself.

Truly BioSafe

Free from PFAS, microplastics and harmful surfactants. Safe for staff, guests, pets and surfaces in contact with food.

§ 05 — The genesis

Cleaning, reimagined
from the molecule up.

Atranga was founded on a quiet, almost paradoxical idea: the most powerful cleaning agents shouldn't be hostile to the world they're cleaning. Water-based, biodegradable, biosafe.

Every formulation is developed at the company's own R&D laboratory — tested on real surfaces, in real working environments. From luxury yachts crossing the Mediterranean to greenhouses in the Westland, the same principle holds: clean deeply, protect quietly, leave nothing harmful behind.
